Lesson 2 of 12

Read the Drawing Key / Legend

Before counting anything, read the key. It tells you what each symbol means, its abbreviation, and which color/category it belongs to.

LOOK FOR
  • Device abbreviation
  • Device symbol
  • Wall vs. ceiling indication
  • Color/category

Mounting location is part of the key. Attachment line = wall-mounted. Circle = ceiling-mounted. A Wall Speaker/Strobe and a Ceiling Speaker/Strobe do a similar job, but they are counted as separate takeoff categories.

Do not start counting until you understand the key.

Lesson 3 of 12

Read the Drawing Notes

Read all applicable notes right after the key. Notes can change how a symbol is interpreted — what's new vs. existing, what's excluded, or what it connects to.

LOOK FOR
  • Required mounting
  • Device spacing
  • Manufacturer requirements
  • Special interfaces

Key first. Notes second. Count third.

Identify the Products

Know the product category behind each symbol before you count it. The Academy exercise asks what you're counting; Sales Engineering confirms the final SKU later.

Mounting location can also change the category — a wall-mounted device and a ceiling-mounted device with a similar function are still counted separately.

Apply the Color System

Ban-Koe colors make a takeoff easier to scan and audit. Smoke Detectors are RED, Heat Detectors are LIGHT BLUE, Pull Stations are GREEN — see the full Ban-Koe Takeoff Color System below for every category.

Lesson 6 of 12

Count Systematically

Start at a known location and work through the drawing in one consistent pattern until every applicable device has been considered exactly once.

LOOK FOR
  • Every occurrence of the symbol
  • Rooms you already counted
  • Devices near doors/walls that are easy to miss
  • Ceiling-mounted devices

Review the Counted Marks

Once a device is counted, its mark stays visible on the drawing. Use those marks with your tally to avoid double-counting or missing one.

Identify → Count → Move to the next device.

Reconcile Your Takeoff

Compare your final category quantities against the drawing takeoff and identify discrepancies. If something doesn't match, go back to the drawing and recount.

Count → Tally → Enter → Reconcile.

Common Takeoff Mistakes

  • Counting before reading the key or notes
  • Misidentifying symbols or mixing product types
  • Double-counting or missing devices
  • Entering the wrong category or quantity
  • Failing to reconcile the final tally

  • The duct detector must provide a signal to the fire alarm system
