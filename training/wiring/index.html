Step 1

Know Your Circuits

SLC

Signaling Line Circuit

An SLC is used for communication with addressable initiating devices and other compatible addressable devices on the system.

NAC

Notification Appliance Circuit

A NAC is used to provide power/output to notification appliances such as horns, strobes, or horn/strobes, depending on the system design.

Key Difference

SLC

Primarily communication / device signaling.

NAC

Primarily notification output.

Category SLC NAC
Purpose Addressable device communication Notification output
Typical devices Detectors, pull stations, modules Horns, strobes, horn/strobes, speakers
Circuit function Signaling and status reporting Output activation for occupant alerting
Conceptual difference Input and communication path Output and notification path

Understanding Isolators

An isolator is used in certain addressable systems to help isolate a shorted section of the circuit so that the rest of the loop can continue operating, depending on the system architecture.
