    Useful Questions to Ask
    • What system are you using?
    • Do you have the drawings/specifications?
    • What quantity is required?
    • What is the intended function?
    • Does this interface with another building system?
    • What exact condition needs to trigger the action?
    • Is there an existing device/module?
    • Has the product compatibility been verified?
    • What accessories or power requirements apply?
    • Is there anything else on the plans that affects the BOM?
