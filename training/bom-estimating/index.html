    Estimating Concepts

    Quantity: How many units are required.

    Unit Price: Price for one unit.

    Extended Price: Quantity × Unit Price.

    Subtotal: Total of material line items.

    Markup: Additional amount applied according to company pricing policy.

    Sell Price: The amount presented to the customer.

      What You Learned

      • A takeoff identifies project requirements.
      • A BOM organizes those requirements into materials.
      • Product selection must be verified.
      • Quantities must be checked.
      • Modules/interfaces can be easy to overlook.
      • Power and accessories may need to be considered.
      • Pricing must be based on current approved pricing.
      • A BOM should be reviewed before quoting.
      • Revisions and project documentation matter.

      Quick Reference

      Estimating Flow

      • TAKEOFFWhat does the project require?
      • BOMWhat materials fulfill those requirements?
      • QUANTITYHow many?
      • PRODUCTWhich exact verified item?
      • PRICEWhat is the approved current price?
      • REVIEWDid I miss anything?
      • QUOTEIs the material list ready for pricing/quotation?
