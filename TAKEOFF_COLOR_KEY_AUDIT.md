## Academy Standard

- DARK BLUE: FACP, FAA
- RED: Smokes
- LIGHT BLUE: Heat Detectors
- GREEN: Pulls
- PINK: CR's, CT-1, CT-2, Waterflow, Door Holders, Tamper
- ORANGE: Duct Smokes, Test Stations, Wall Strobes, Ceiling Strobes
- YELLOW: Wall Horn/Strobes, Wall Speaker/Strobes, Ceiling Horn/Strobes, Ceiling Speaker/Strobes, Weatherproof Devices

## Source Conflict Handling

The 9 orange `S` symbols in Exercise 001 are **UNRESOLVED / EXCLUDED FROM SCORING**. They are not classified as Wall Strobes, Wall Speaker/Strobes, or another category. The source PNG remains visually untouched, and the learner is not penalized for leaving them uncounted.
