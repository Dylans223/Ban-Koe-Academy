## Source Rules

- These mappings connect verified Academy product identities to Takeoff categories only; they do not alter Product Library records.
- A module that can interface with a field device is not automatically the part number for that field-device category.
- `E850...` and `E85...` identifiers found in source URLs, document titles, or catalog records remain document identifiers, not part numbers.
- The confirmed Takeoff colors were supplied separately by the user and are not claimed to have been established by the original Takeoffs PDF.
